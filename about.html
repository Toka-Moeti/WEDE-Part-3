Organisation History

Concept Media is a creative agency and talent management company founded in 2024. Born from a collaboration between experienced event partners, we created a unified brand to deliver bigger cultural experiences with wider reach.

We began by hosting the Flavius TVET Freshers, then expanded with Games Nights, business competitions, and social events across Sasolburg.

Today, our flagship property Campus Clash positions us at the forefront of student culture, while our artist booking division manages talent, fees, and logistics for brands and events.

Concept Media exists to create moments that move people, elevate brands, and shape youth culture.
